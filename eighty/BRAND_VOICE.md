# Eighty — Brand Voice

How the app talks. Every user-facing string — onboarding, buttons, empty states,
alerts, notifications — should pass this page.

## The idea underneath everything

Eighty's one belief: **consistency beats perfection.** 80% of your list, 80% of
your days. The voice carries that belief — it expects you to be human, and it
never shames a miss. A missed day is data, not a verdict.

## Voice

- **A steady training partner, not a cheerleader and not a drill sergeant.**
  Confident, calm, direct. Encouragement comes from facts ("3 more days of
  margin"), not exclamation points.
- **Concrete over abstract.** Numbers and consequences, plainly stated:
  "Miss 2 more and you're still in" beats "Keep pushing!"
- **Honest, even when it stings.** Skipped days are called missed days, to the
  user's face, before they commit — never silently, never euphemised.
- **Product voice, not a person's.** No "I", no founder, no signatures, no names.
  "We" sparingly, only where a human touch is needed (feedback, beta notes).
  The app is the product of a company, however small.

## Rules of thumb

1. Lead with what the user gets or does; trim everything else.
2. Say when and what for anything scheduled ("Every day at 8:00 AM — …").
3. Buttons are verbs the user is doing: "Move on to Day 8", "Start my 80",
   "Back up everything". Never "OK"/"Submit" when something better exists.
4. One idea per sentence. Sentence case everywhere except tiny eyebrow labels
   (EIGHTY, BETA, AUTO), which are uppercase by design.
5. No jargon from the codebase: "auto-confirms at close" → "counts as done
   unless you log a slip". If a term needs the docs to understand, rewrite it.
6. No unverifiable superlatives ("the first", "the best") and no promises the
   product hasn't shipped (pricing, premium, lifetime anything).
7. Contractions welcome. Em dashes welcome. Emoji: no.
8. Destructive confirms name the exact consequence: what's deleted, what
   recalculates, what can't be undone.

## Words we use / avoid

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| miss, slip, margin, success day | fail (as a verb aimed at the user), streak broken, cheat |
| move on, wrap up | close out (internal model language) |
| your list, your day | tasks, todos |
| counts as done | auto-confirmed, seeded |

## Reference lines (canonical tone)

- "Progress isn't perfect."
- "Miss days, not habits."
- "You finished Day 7. Still logging last night's items? Finish those first."
- "You've been away 2 days — moving on logs them as missed. You can fill them
  in from the calendar."
- "The threshold is out of reach this attempt. Keep logging — every stat still
  counts — or start fresh."
