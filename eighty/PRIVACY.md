# Eighty — Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 17, 2026

## The short version

Eighty keeps everything on your device. There is no account, no server, no analytics,
and no tracking. The app does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
information — because none of your data ever leaves your phone unless *you* choose to
export it.

## What the app stores (on your device only)

Everything you enter lives in a local database on your iPhone:

- Your challenge setups (names, durations, thresholds, checklists).
- Your daily check-offs and which days you closed out.
- Optional satisfaction and mood ratings, travel-day flags, and notes.
- Your app settings (theme, reminder preferences).

None of this is uploaded anywhere. There is no login and no cloud sync in this version.

## Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Eighty schedules **local** notifications on your device
(a morning "close out yesterday" nudge and an evening "unchecked items" nudge). These
are generated on your phone. No notification is sent through us or any third party, and
turning them off in the app (or in iOS Settings) stops them.

## Backups and exports

Eighty lets you export your data to a file so you can back it up or move it to another
device. When you tap "Back up everything" or export a single challenge, iOS's standard
share sheet lets you choose where that file goes (Files, AirDrop, email, etc.). That file
goes wherever **you** send it. The developer never receives it and has no access to it.

Restoring or importing a file reads it back into the app on your device only.

## Third parties

Eighty contains no third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs. It makes no
network requests to send your data anywhere.

## Children's privacy

Eighty does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a future version, the updated policy will be posted at this
