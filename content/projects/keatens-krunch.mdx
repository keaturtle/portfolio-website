## Overview

Keaten's Krunch is a small business I started to highlight nonprofits through the sale of homemade granola. A portion of every sale goes directly to a featured nonprofit organization.

## Mission

To create awareness and funding for nonprofits by connecting them with customers through a tangible, delicious product.

## What I Learned

Running Keaten's Krunch taught me about product development, marketing, supply chain management, and the challenges of running a small business while balancing other commitments.
