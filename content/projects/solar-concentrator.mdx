## Overview

As part of ME 328, I designed an efficient and economical solar concentrator that could be easily built by communities in need of low-cost energy solutions.

## My Contributions

- Contributed a full SolidWorks CAD model of the concentrator assembly
- Performed design calculations for focal length, reflector geometry, and thermal output
- Optimized the design for manufacturability using locally available materials

## Design Process

The concentrator uses a parabolic reflector to focus sunlight onto a receiver at the focal point. Key design constraints included low material cost, ease of fabrication, and sufficient thermal output for cooking or water heating applications.

## Results

The final design achieved the target concentration ratio while remaining within the cost and fabrication constraints set by the project brief.
