## Overview

Utana Tech was a product startup I co-founded after winning a pitch competition. We carried out the full product development process from concept to prototype.

## The Journey

- Won a university pitch competition with the initial concept
- Conducted market research and customer discovery interviews
- Developed product specifications and initial prototypes
- Built out the company structure and go-to-market strategy

## Key Takeaways

This experience gave me hands-on exposure to the full product development lifecycle, from ideation through prototyping and business planning.
